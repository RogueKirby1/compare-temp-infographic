What half a degree changes

Switch between two worlds: one where warming levels off at 1.5°C above 1850–1900, and one where it reaches 2.0°C.

These are not forecasts for a particular year. When the IPCC wrote its 2018 report, warming was about 1°C, and it said 1.5°C would likely arrive between 2030 and 2052 at the then-current rate.
